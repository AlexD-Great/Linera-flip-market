========================================
STEP 6: Build WASM Binaries
========================================

Copy and paste these commands into your Ubuntu terminal:

cd /mnt/c/Users/shelby/Desktop/Linera-flip-market
cargo build --release --target wasm32-unknown-unknown

This will create:
- target/wasm32-unknown-unknown/release/flip_market_contract.wasm
- target/wasm32-unknown-unknown/release/flip_market_service.wasm

Time: ~5-10 minutes

========================================
STEP 7: Deploy to Testnet Conway
========================================

Initialize wallet (first time only):

linera wallet init --with-new-chain

Deploy the application:

linera project publish-and-create .

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Save the Application ID that appears!

Start the service:

linera service --port 8080

Your application will be live at:
http://localhost:8080/chains/<CHAIN_ID>/applications/<APP_ID>

Time: ~2-5 minutes
