========================================
STEP 5: Build Linera CLI
========================================

⚠️ WARNING: This step takes 15-30 minutes!

Copy and paste these commands into your Ubuntu terminal:

cd ~/linera-protocol
cargo build --release -p linera-service

After it completes, add Linera to your PATH:

echo 'export PATH="$HOME/linera-protocol/target/release:$PATH"' >> ~/.bashrc
source ~/.bashrc

Verify installation:

linera --version

You should see: linera x.x.x

Time: ~15-30 minutes (be patient!)
